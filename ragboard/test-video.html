🎯 Usage Instructions

  1. From Sidebar: Click the video icon in the left sidebar
  2. Add Video URL: Paste YouTube URL or direct video file URL
  3. Upload File: Drag & drop video files or click to browse
  4. Set Title: Add a descriptive title for the video
  5. Place on Board: Video node will appear on the canvas
  6. Interact: Resize, move, connect to other nodes
